CAB-01 · why Charlie's

The body shop that
doesn't need to sell you.

Collision repair is an industry where customers often feel uncertain. You are not sure what actually needs to be fixed, whether the shop is recommending unnecessary work, or if the price is fair. Charlie's was built on eliminating that uncertainty.

We tell you what we find, explain what needs to be done, and give you an honest estimate. No pressure, no upsells, no surprises. That approach has earned us something that cannot be manufactured: the long-term trust of the people we serve.

Customers come back not because of a promotion or a guarantee, but because they know from experience that the work will be done properly and the experience will be straightforward. Many have been with us for years. Some for decades.

CAB-02 · what we do

Every repair,
the same standard.

From major collision damage to a small paint touch-up, every job gets the same attention to detail. Here is what we handle:

Collision Repair — Full accident repair from start to finish. We assess structural and cosmetic damage, source the right parts, and restore your vehicle to its pre-accident condition. Every panel fits correctly, every surface is finished properly.

Auto Body Repair — Dings, scratches, and the general wear of daily driving. We correct panel damage, fix alignment issues, and restore your vehicle's body lines to clean and consistent.

Paint & Refinishing — Color matching, blending, and finish work done with care. Whether it is a full panel repaint or a small touch-up, we focus on accuracy and a finish that holds up.

Bumper Repair & Replacement — One of the most common jobs we handle. Scuffs, cracks, and impact damage repaired, or replacement bumpers sourced, painted, and installed with proper fitment.

Dent & Cosmetic Repair — Small dents and surface imperfections addressed without unnecessary expense. We evaluate each one individually to determine the best approach.

Light & Part Replacement — Broken tail lights, damaged mirrors, worn trim. Parts sourced efficiently, painted when necessary, and installed properly. Many of these smaller jobs are completed quickly.
