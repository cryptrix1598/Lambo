GCS-01 · why us

The foreign car shop that
doesn't need to sell you.

Foreign and German car repair is an area where dealerships often overcharge and independent shops under-deliver. You are not sure what actually needs to be fixed, whether the shop knows your BMW or Mercedes well enough, or if the price is fair. German Car Services was built on eliminating that uncertainty.

We tell you what we find, explain what needs to be done, and give you an honest estimate. No pressure, no upsells, no surprises. That approach has earned us something that cannot be manufactured: the long-term trust of the people we serve.

GCS-02 · what we do

Every car,
the same standard.

From engine diagnostics to brake service, every job gets the same attention to detail. Here is what we handle:

BMW & Mercedes-Benz Service — Engine diagnostics, maintenance, and repair for BMW and Mercedes-Benz vehicles. Our mechanics know these cars inside and out, from routine service to complex repairs. The right diagnostic tools, the right parts, the right fix.

Audi & Porsche Service — Audi and Porsche vehicles demand specialist knowledge. We service and repair these cars with the expertise that keeps them running the way they were built to run. No guesswork, no shortcuts.

Volkswagen Service — From the Golf to the Touareg, we handle Volkswagen repair and maintenance with the same care and expertise we bring to every European car in the shop.

Brake Service & Repair — Brake pads, rotors, calipers, and fluid. We inspect, repair, and replace braking systems with the attention to detail that safety demands. No worn parts leave the shop.

Engine Diagnostics & Repair — Modern German cars run on complex electronics and software. We use proper diagnostic equipment to find the real problem, not just the one the dashboard light suggests.

General Foreign & Domestic Repair — German cars are our specialty, but we work on all makes and models. Oil changes, suspension work, exhaust repair, electrical issues, and the everyday maintenance that keeps any car on the road.
