Our Services

Charlie's Auto Body Shop provides a complete range of auto body and collision repair services. Whether your vehicle was involved in a major accident or needs a minor cosmetic touch-up, our team has the skill and experience to restore it properly. Every repair is handled with the same attention to detail — because we believe the quality of the work speaks for itself.

CAB-COL

Collision Repair

When your vehicle has been in an accident, the repair process can feel overwhelming. We handle the entire collision repair process from start to finish — assessing structural and cosmetic damage, sourcing the right parts, and restoring your vehicle to its pre-accident condition. Our team works carefully to ensure every panel fits correctly, every surface is finished properly, and the final result meets our standards before we consider the job complete.

CAB-BDY

Auto Body Repair

Beyond collision damage, vehicles accumulate body wear over time — dings from parking lots, scratches from road debris, and the general effects of daily driving. Our auto body repair service addresses these issues with precision. We work on all makes and models, restoring damaged panels, correcting alignment issues, and ensuring your vehicle's body lines are clean and consistent.

CAB-PAINT

Paint & Refinishing

A quality paint job is what separates a good repair from a great one. We handle paint matching, refinishing, and touch-up work with care — blending new paint into existing surfaces so the result looks seamless. Whether the work involves a full panel repaint or a small touch-up, we focus on color accuracy, even coverage, and a finish that holds up over time.

CAB-BMP

Bumper Repair & Replacement

Bumper damage is one of the most common body repair needs, and it is one of the jobs we handle most frequently. We repair scuffs, cracks, and impact damage on existing bumpers, or source and install replacement bumpers when the damage is beyond repair. Parts are obtained efficiently, painted to match, and installed with proper fitment — usually faster than you would expect.

CAB-DENT

Dent & Cosmetic Repair

Small dents, dings, and surface imperfections can affect the overall appearance of your vehicle. Our cosmetic repair service addresses these issues without unnecessary expense. We evaluate each dent individually to determine the best approach — whether that means careful repair of the existing surface or a more involved body correction. The goal is always the same: a clean result that looks right.

CAB-LIGHT

Light & Part Replacement

Broken tail lights, damaged mirrors, and worn trim pieces are small problems that can make a big difference in how your vehicle looks and functions. We source replacement parts efficiently, paint them when necessary, and install them properly. Many of these smaller jobs are completed quickly, getting you back on the road without a long wait.
